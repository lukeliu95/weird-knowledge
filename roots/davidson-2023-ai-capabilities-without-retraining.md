# 不重新训练也能大幅提升 AI 能力（训练后增强）（AI capabilities can be significantly improved without expensive retraining）

## 一句话

把工具调用、提示方法、脚手架、答案挑选、数据生成五类训练后增强折算成“等效训练算力增益”；Epoch 的介绍称部分方法相当于多用 5～20 倍训练算力，成本却不到 1%。

## 原始出处

- AI capabilities can be significantly improved without expensive retraining, Tom Davidson et al., 2023, arXiv:2312.07413，https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07413
- Epoch AI 对该论文的介绍，https://epoch.ai/blog/ai-capabilities-can-be-significantly-improved-without-expensive-retraining
