# 95-5 法则（多数 B2B 买方此刻不在市场上）（Advertising effectiveness and the 95-5 rule: most B2B buyers are not in the market right now）

## 一句话

企业约每 5 年才换一次主要供应商，所以任一季度只有约 5% 的买方在市场上，广告的作用是给其余 95% 建立品牌记忆，等他们进场时想得起你。

## 原始出处

- Advertising effectiveness and the 95-5 rule: most B2B buyers are not in the market right now，John Dawes，Ehrenberg-Bass Institute B2B Report，2021-05，https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/advertising-effectiveness-and-the-95-5-rule-most-b2b-buyers-are-not-in-the-market-right-now
- 同报告 PDF 副本，https://www.93x.agency/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/advertising-effectiveness-and-the-95-5-rule.pdf
- 争议：作者自己说 95% 不是精确规律，只是按平均换购周期推算的启发式；各品类应按自己的换购周期重算。
