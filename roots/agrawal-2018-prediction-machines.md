# 《预测机器：人工智能的简单经济学》（Prediction Machines: The Simple Economics of Artificial Intelligence）

## 一句话

AI 的本质是让“预测”变便宜；预测变便宜后，人的预测这种替代品贬值，而判断、行动、数据这类互补品升值。

## 原始出处

- Prediction, Judgment and Complexity, Agrawal, Gans & Goldfarb, NBER Working Paper 24243, 2018，https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24243/w24243.pdf
- The Economics of AI: Prediction Machines and Their Impact with Ajay Agrawal, Technology Policy Institute 访谈，https://techpolicyinstitute.org/publications/artificial-intelligence/the-economics-of-ai-prediction-machines-and-their-impact-with-ajay-agrawal/
