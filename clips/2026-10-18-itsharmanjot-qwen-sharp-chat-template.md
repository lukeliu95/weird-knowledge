## 原文

Local models just got a free intelligence upgrade.

Someone took the new Qwen3.8-27B (the one that already runs in 22GB) and only changed the chat template.

No fine-tune. No new training. Just a smarter system prompt that kills all the filler and forces the model to stay sharp.

Result: the same weights are now fixing real post-cutoff bugs faster and cleaner than Claude Opus 5 High in their tests.

They call it the Sharp template.

It’s free. Drop it on any Qwen3.5 / 3.6 / 3.8 model and it just works with llama.cpp.

This is the kind of quiet upgrade that actually moves the needle for people running models locally.
