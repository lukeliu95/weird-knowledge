## 原文

🚨 JUST IN: @Microsoft released Mage, a 4B open-weight computer-use agent that scores 66% on OSWorld-Verified.

Humans score 72.4%.

One spreadsheet task took Mage 39 seconds. Qwen3.6-27B, a model almost 6x bigger, needed nearly 10 minutes when limited to clicking.

Mage switches between clicking like a person and typing commands, step by step.

On OSWorld-Verified, Mage also beats Qwen3.6-27B, 66% to 63.9%.

Anyone can download and use it, even commercially. At 9.5GB, Mage fits easily on my 64GB M5 Pro Mac mini. 

Testing it tonight!
