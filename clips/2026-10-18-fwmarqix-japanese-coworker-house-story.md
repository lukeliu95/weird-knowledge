## 原文

My Japanese coworker died. I barely knew him. Went to the funeral to be polite.

His BROTHER approached me after.

Brother: Kenji left you something.

Me: What? Why?

Brother: hands me keys, His house.

Me: I'M SORRY, WHAT?!

Brother: He had no children. Will says "to the foreigner at my company who always microwaved strange food."

Me: I microwaved LEFTOVERS!

Brother: Apparently that impressed him.

The house was in Saitama. 3 bedrooms. Fully paid off.

Worth ¥25 million (~$170k USD).

Me: This is insane! I can't accept this!

Lawyer: You legally have to. It's in the will.

Me: WHAT IF I REFUSE?

Lawyer: Then it goes to the government.

Me: ...I'll take the house.

Week 1: The house was FULL of stuff. Old furniture, books, boxes.

I started going through it.

Found a box labeled "FOR THE FOREIGNER."

Inside: 40 Tupperware containers and a note.

"You clearly don't have proper food storage. You're welcome. -Kenji"

Me: crying, He left me TUPPERWARE?!

Month 2: Found another box: "ALSO FOR THE FOREIGNER."

Inside: A rice cooker, proper knives, and a 10-page handwritten guide on "How to Not Embarrass Yourself in Japan."

Me: He was HELPING me from beyond the grave!

Month 4: I found a THIRD box: "FINAL GIFT."

Inside: ¥3 million in cash and a note.

"Taxes on the house will be expensive. This should cover 5 years. Don't be stupid with it. -Kenji"

Me: absolutely losing it

Month 6: I moved into the house full time.

The neighbors welcomed me.

Neighbor: Kenji-san talked about you.

Me: He did?!

Neighbor: He said "The foreigner is helpless but trying."

Me: THAT'S what he said?!

Neighbor: He liked you. That's why he left you the house.

Me: We NEVER had a conversation!

Neighbor: Kenji didn't need conversations. He observed.

Present day:

I still live in Kenji's house.

I use his Tupperware every day.

I followed his 10-page guide.

Last week I found ANOTHER box: "IF YOU'RE STILL HERE."

Inside: A bottle of whiskey and a note.

"Good. You stayed. The house suits you. Take care of it. -Kenji"

I poured a glass for him.

Best coworker I never really knew.
