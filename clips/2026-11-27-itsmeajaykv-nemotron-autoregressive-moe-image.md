## 原文

Never thought i'd see an autogressive MoE image-gen model !

@NVIDIAAI Nemotron-Labs-Image-30B-A3B !

30B model with just 3B active parameters !

Unlike conventional diffusion models that repeatedly de-noise an entire image, this generates visual tokens (pixel as code) just like an LLM !

This means a lot for local AI, we can leverage years of LLM inference optimizations, quantizations and expert offloading to make image generation more accessible on consumer hardware.

Exciting times ahead !

Kudos to @NVIDIAAI for all open source work.
