## 原文

Introducing Vivix-W1, a streaming-native multimodal model.

While many interactive models already let you navigate generated worlds through camera controls, W1 opens up more ways to interact in real time: touch what you see, direct characters, and shape what happens next with voice, text, images, or video.

Imagine tapping a character on the shoulder and saying hi, they turn around, smile, and greet you.

W1 natively understands text, images, audio, video,and interaction context as references. The references aren’t limited to frame one: they can enter mid-stream and become part of the current scene, rather than forcing you to generate a separate clip.

There's a director built into the model. W1 natively generates video and audio together, directing story, cinematography, and sound as one. It moves the story forward across multiple shots, deciding when to cut, shift viewpoints, or move the camera while keeping dialogue, action, and sound in sync.
