## 原文

State-of-the-art voice isolation with Inworld Voice Isolator @inworld is now supported in audio.cpp v0.9.6.

Street noise, background music, crowd chatter—this model does an astonishing job of isolating the foreground voice while preserving its character, breaths, and laughter.

Now available in audio.cpp:

🎙️ Offline processing for recordings
⚡ Real-time streaming for live audio
🎵 48 kHz output
📦 GGUF models running natively in C++
💻 Local inference across popular backends, including WebGPU!

Turn the sound on and hear it for yourself. This cleanup is absolutely incredible. 🤯🔊
