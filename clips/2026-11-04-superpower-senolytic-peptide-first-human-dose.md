## 原文

BREAKING: A peptide that targets “zombie cells” has just been given to a human for the first time.

As you age, your body builds up so-called “zombie cells.”

These are old, damaged cells that stop dividing but don’t die.

They’ve been linked to cancer and age-related diseases like Alzheimer’s, heart disease and type 2 diabetes.

The peptide is called SenoMune-0257.

In young people, the immune system efficiently clears these cells before they accumulate, but immune surveillance declines with age, allowing them to persist.

SenoMune-0257 is designed to train your immune system to find and clear them.

The first patient got it last week in Mexico, as part of a study of people with advanced cancer.

What still needs to be shown is whether it works in people.
Still, the biology behind it looks promising.

Imagine a drug that could help with cancer, Alzheimer’s, heart disease and diabetes at the same time.
