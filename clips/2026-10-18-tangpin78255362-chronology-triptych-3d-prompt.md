prompt:

Present an exquisite, miniature 3D cartoon-style triptych illustrating the chronology of the user-specified subject/topic, clearly viewed from a 45° top-down perspective. The triptych is divided into three vertical sections from left to right, each representing a major chronological stage.

For each chronological stage, place the subject’s/topic's most iconic representation, character, or landmark prominently at the center, complemented by proportionally-sized icons of key elements, symbolic items, charming figures, props, and other details illustrating the stage’s core theme or narrative. The stage should be detailed, finely crafted, and playful.

Rendered with Cinema 4D, the modeling should be refined, smoothly rounded, and rich in texture, accurately capturing realistic PBR materials. Gentle, lifelike lighting and soft shadows should create a warm, comfortable ambiance.

Maintain a clean, minimalist layout and a solid-color background from the subject/topic’s color palette to highlight the primary content.

At the top-center of the triptych, prominently display the subject/topic name in a large font size. Each stage should come with a short caption.

All texts should be displayed in the language specified or entered by the user, without any background, and may subtly overlap with the scene elements to enhance overall design integration.

Subject Name / Topic: {subject/topic}
