## 原文

Four months after we launched Manus, I was invited to a dinner with President Macron. I ended up seated directly across from Jensen Huang.

He was half an hour late, and Macron asked him to give a toast. Jensen picked up his wine glass and turned it into a fully improvised speech — in front of a table of European politicians and founders. It was electric. The charisma of a top founder, on full display.

I typed the whole thing into my Notes that night. Still one of the sharpest takes I’ve heard on software, AI and Europe.

What a pity. What a loss.
