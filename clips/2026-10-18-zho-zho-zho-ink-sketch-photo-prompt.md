## 原文

这组提示词太酷了！！！

优化了一下原作者的 Prompt：这样保持和原图完全一样的姿势和构图（原提示词仅保持了面部的一致，会导致构图和姿势不一致⬇️

Create a photo-style line drawing / ink sketch of the person identical to the uploaded image — keep every facial feature, proportion, and expression exactly the same.
Use blue and white ink tones with intricate, fine line detailing, drawn on a notebook-page style background.
Show a right hand holding a pen and an eraser near the sketch, as if the artist is still working.
