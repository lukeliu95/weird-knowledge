## 原文

A 21-year-old guy from Brazil created an AI construction worker girl, and 764 men bought a paid subscription to see her

Subscription price: $9.99
Total revenue: $7,632

Investment: $200 spent on Claude for generation

He chose a niche that wasn't crowded and made the right call: the "construction girl" persona really caught men's attention

At first, he posted videos of her on TikTok, and once he built an audience, he started promoting his Fanvue page, where the paid subscription (and some slightly different content, if you know what I mean) was hosted

It took him 37 days to set it all up. He easily recouped his investment and reached a solid profit

AI is a useful tool, but first and foremost, you need to have a good imagination
