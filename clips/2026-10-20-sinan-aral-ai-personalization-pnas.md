## 原文

🚨Out now in PNAS! The first ever (we believe) large scale RCT + field experiment on the performance effects of AI Personalization!

 🚩 In a preregistered randomized experiment, 
 🚩 We paired 1,258 participants with AI agents prompted to exhibit varying levels of the Big Five personality traits. 
 🚩 These human–AI teams produced 7,266 display ads, evaluated by 1,168 human raters, and a field experiment on X with ~5M impressions.

---

TLDR?

 🚩 Human and AI personalities shaped ad quality and teamwork.
 🚩  Human–AI personality pairings directly influenced ad quality.
 🚩 Ad quality significantly influenced ad CTR and CPC.
 🚩 Together, the results show AI personalization improves human–AI collaboration performance.

---

More specifically:

1) Human and AI personalities predicted performance:

 🚩 Conscientious and agreeable humans experienced stronger task and social teamwork and performed better on all six underlying teamwork dimensions.
 🚩 Human openness predicted higher text quality and human extraversion predicted lower text quality. 
 🚩 AI extraversion increased text quality, whereas AI conscientiousness reduced text quality.

---

2) Beyond individual traits, human-AI personality pairings significantly influenced ad quality:

 🚩 The largest three effects on the composite human-rated ad-quality score were for extraverted humans paired with conscientious AI, conscientious humans paired with agreeable AI, and neurotic humans paired with conscientious AI.
 🚩 Extraverted humans paired with conscientious AI lowered quality across all three measured dimensions including text, image, and click likelihood, while pairing with extraverted AI increased text quality. 
 🚩 Conscientious humans produced lower quality text when paired with agreeable AI and higher quality text and images when paired with conscientious and open AI. 
 🚩 Open humans produced higher quality text when paired with conscientious AI and lower quality text when paired with agreeable AI. 
 🚩 Agreeable humans produced lower quality text when paired with extraverted AI and lower quality images when paired with open AI. 
 🚩 Finally, neurotic humans produced ads with lower click likelihood when paired with conscientious AI.

---

In the field, ad quality predicted field performance:

 🚩 A 1 SD increase in text quality predicted a 7.0% increase in CTR.
 🚩 A 1 SD increase in image quality predicted a 3.9% (or $0.30) reduction in CPC.

Bottom line?

 🚩 The pairing effect on quality translates to a ∼2.7% improvement in CTR and a ∼1.5% improvement in CPC. These are big effects for advertising.
 🚩 Taken together, these results suggest the existence of economically and statistically significant gains from AI personalization.
