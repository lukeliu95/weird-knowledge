## 原文

Hate to break it to you, but the global hardware shortage has not even started yet.

Right now, only 1% of humanity is burning any real amount of AI tokens. The rest are just using free accounts once in a while to ask dumb questions like "Who would win in a fight between a baby seal and 300 hamsters?"

And like Elon said, most RAM will not even go into data centers. It will go into Physical AI, which is robotics.

That is because robotics is the only thing that can actually boost human productivity in a real way.

People on X freak out every time AI solves a hard math problem, while people in the real world just go on with their lives like nothing happened. Meanwhile, robotics is already taking over factories and changing the physical world.

Imagine when these robots start replacing our daily chores. How much more RAM and compute will we need then?

Like I have said many times, building a new fab to make RAM takes at least 5 years, and supply is already fully booked out until 2029.

Even if the so-called AI bubble pops, this massive RAM shortage is not going away.

Starting next year, even the new iPhone might be too expensive for most people to afford.
