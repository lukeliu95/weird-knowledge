## 原文

🇺🇸 🇨🇳 China wired its grid at 800 kilovolts decades ago... and that one decision is now squeezing American AI.

Economist Steve Keen says higher voltage lets China pipe cheap power thousands of kilometers to remote data centers, while US ones sit near cities paying more, leaving Americans facing Lamborghini prices for AI:

“I can see the Chinese [version] being, you know, 90% of the functionality, 10% of the cost.”

Chinese data centers already pay about a third of what US ones pay for electricity, so the AI race may come down to the power bill.
