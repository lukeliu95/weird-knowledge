## 原文

Please stop personalizing your emails...

We tested AI written first lines against leaving them out.

The AI lines hit a 0.27% reply rate.

Emails with nothing but a first name hit 10%+.

What works is real data in a fixed template:

> Variables filled from their data
> No first name? Drop the greeting
> QA every email before it sends

The rest of the relevance comes from the list and the offer.
