## 原文

Your model should dream while you sleep. 😴

Today we’re open-sourcing Songe.

It’s a local training engine that turns your conversations, instructions, and corrections into training data, then fine-tunes your local model on your own tasks and preferences.

It all runs on your own machine, using compute that would otherwise sit idle.

Your data stays on your device. The model that gets better is yours.
