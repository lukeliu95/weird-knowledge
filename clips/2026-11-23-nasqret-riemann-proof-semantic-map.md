## 原文

A world map of the proof of the Riemann hypothesis!Introducing a fully interactive map of the semantic embeddings of all the Lean declarations and proofs in the formalization of the Riemann hypothesis.

You can explore the whole proof visually, see where the declarations live and how they connect and interact, search the map, and follow links to the original source code. The map will be updated with each iteration of the proof.

If you want to focus on one aspect of this monumental proof, you can now locate semantically related fragments. I hope this will help structure the code and broaden our understanding of its high- and low-level structure.
