## 原文

H3 Max Director 1.1 is now available.

New features for longer, more controllable video generation:

- Start with any frame and insert keyframes throughout a generation
- Introduce audio at any point, with native audio conditioning
- Generate natively in 1080p
- Run continuous generations for up to 15 minutes

Director adapts to new visual and audio inputs in real time, giving you precise control without breaking continuity.
