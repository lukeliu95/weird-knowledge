## 原文

Today we’re introducing Tofu-1, Bland’s speech-to-speech model.

The highest-stakes calls rarely happen in good conditions: from moving cars and hospital rooms, over weak signal, with several people talking at once.

Tofu-1 is built for those calls. It listens while it speaks, reasons while it talks, and can tell a caller apart from everything else on a line, like a TV or a crying baby.
