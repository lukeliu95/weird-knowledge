Announcing H3 Max Turbo (preview)

H3 Max Turbo is a faster, more cost-efficient version of H3 Max. 

It runs at 2x the speed at half the cost, while targeting the 97th percentile of the original H3 Max's quality on our evals (and still performing better than H3 and any other video model). At our promotional price, 768p video runs at $0.01 per second of output.

H3 Max Turbo keeps the prompt understanding, aesthetics, and overall quality of H3 Max while cutting generation time in half. 

It’s available to try at a promotional price for the next two weeks. See what happens when video generation gets an order of magnitude faster and cheaper.
